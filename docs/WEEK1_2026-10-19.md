## Decisions made while building (flag if wrong)

- **Modal gating.** The disclosure line in the modal renders on every bag (it is a fact about the
  brand); only the primary-button swap and the why-chosen restyle are gated to `golf-sim`, per the memo.
- **Exit verb.** `link.label` wins when set, else `getLinkCTA()` with the trailing "on" dropped, so the
  row reads "Shop · carlofet.com" rather than "Shop on · carlofet.com". Seed labels on non-StowGolf
  items are product names ("Rapsodo MLM2PRO · rapsodo.com"); rename the labels to verbs if that reads
  wrong.
- **Spec pairs** hide `list_price` / `founders_price` (the price cell owns money) and format `_ft` /
  `_in` keys with units, so the StowGolf row reads "Width 10 ft · Height 90 in · Depth 5 ft".
- **Subscriptions in the seed** carry the amount in `specs.price_amount` and `price_paid: null`, so the
  modal's "Price Paid" does not show a yearly fee as a one-time purchase.
- **`title: 'Golf Sim'`** for the use case (breadcrumb "Home > Golf Sim", per the evaluation and the
  category label). Only `app/for/[useCase]/page.tsx` reads `useCase.title`.
- **"Join thousands of ..."** was removed from every /for page (the evaluation says "everywhere"); the
  other pages now read "Organize and share your gear with Teed.club." Everything else on them is unchanged.
- **FAQ collapse** uses two renders (`<details>` below `sm`, open cards from `sm`) instead of JS.
- **Bag description** still renders in the quoted italic serif on golf-sim bags; the plain-intro and
  metadata-chip header (design spec section 4) is adopt-order #8 and is left for week 2.
- **Prefixed tags** (`budget:`, `room:`, ...) are not in the seed yet; without the week-2 chip renderer
  they would show as `#budget:under-10k` in the tag row.
- `is_hidden` is filtered on the `/for` fetch (column exists per migration 037); the Discover page does
  not filter it, as before.
