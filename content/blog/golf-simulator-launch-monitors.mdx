## Who this is for

You have a garage bay, a basement or a spare room, you have measured it, and you want to know which launch monitor the room allows before you look at price. Budgets in the threads run from about $500 to about $14,000. We do not rank anything: the order is placement type, then price.

## The sub-shapes

A launch monitor is sorted first by where it sits, because that decides the room.

### Radar

A Doppler radar unit stands 5 to 10 ft behind the golfer, aimed down the target line, and follows the ball out. Makers print about 8 ft of flight as the minimum, so the room runs from 13 ft for the shortest lane to 16 ft or more. Most radar units cost $499.99 to $1,299, and one premium range unit is $4,999. The trade-off is spin: forum owners say radar cannot measure sidespin indoors and estimates it from path and face, so the better units add a camera, marked balls or stickers.

### Camera

A camera (photometric) unit sits on the floor 1 to 2 ft beside the ball and photographs impact. Nothing sits behind the golfer, so every camera unit in the data has a 10 ft minimum depth. Prices run $699 to $4,999.99, and spin is measured. The trade-off is that the unit shares the hitting area, several must be moved to switch handedness, and some need club stickers or marked balls.

### Overhead

An overhead unit mounts on the ceiling, 2 to 3.5 ft in front of the ball or behind the golfer, depending on the model. The floor stays clear, so left and right handers share the bay. You need a 9 to 10 ft ceiling (10 ft 6 in for one Trackman version) and 14 to 16 ft of depth. Prices run $5,999 to $13,995. The trade-off is the install and the money.

## What the forum argues about

How much depth radar needs. Rapsodo and FlightScope print 8 ft of flight; Carl's Place says plan 19 ft or more and Shop Indoor Golf 18 to 20 ft. A 2022 thread settles on 8 ft for irons and about 10 ft for the driver, and another that year says the Mevo+ needs 10 ft of flight for usable spin and 15 ft to shine. Our reading is 16 ft for radar; the 10 ft for camera units is about the ball reaching the screen, not tracking.

The yearly fee is the real price. A Slickdeals buyer called the Launch Pro a great unit apart from the subscription tied to it. Northcourse lists the maker unlock fees side by side, and Foresight+ Gold at $599 plus GSPro at $250 is the number that sends people toward Square, Mevo Gen2 or the EYE MINI LITE. One 2023 poster said Bushnell's basic tier connected to sim software; Bushnell's page says Gold.

Measured or calculated spin. Camera units read spin; radar estimates sidespin, and the cure is a marked ball (Rapsodo RPT, Square, Titleist RCT) or metallic stickers on a Mevo. A 2024 thread traced a GC3's low spin to range balls.

SkyTrak's closed turn. Shop Indoor Golf and Golf Simulator Videos report SkyTrak has stopped officially supporting third-party software. The OpenSkyPlus 2 bridge works but is unsanctioned and tied to app versions 5.4 to 5.8.

Putting and used units. The R10 thread says radar putting is poor beyond about 6 ft, while Square and the Uneekor units are praised for chipping and putting. The forum favorite used is a GC2; ask for the serial and battery history, since subscriptions mostly do not transfer.

## Room depth: the number that decides

Radar needs ball flight in front and a lane behind. Camera units need only the distance from ball to screen. Overhead needs ceiling height and 14 to 16 ft. The data's minimum depths, shortest first:

- **About 10 ft, every camera unit:** R50 (1.5 ft to the side of the tee), Launch Pro (2 ft forward, 1 ft lateral), GC3 (about 2 ft beside), ST MAX (12 to 15 in), SkyTrak+ (11.5 to 12.5 in), EYE MINI LITE (beside, 12 by 8 in zone), EYE MINI (15.75 in), Square Home (42 cm beside, 23 cm back) and Omni (beside).
- **About 13 ft:** SC4 Pro, 5 ft behind plus 8 ft of flight.
- **About 14 ft:** ProTee VX (3 ft 4 in in front at a 9 ft ceiling) and TruGolf APOGEE (21 to 25 in in front).
- **About 15 ft:** Rapsodo MLM2PRO (6.5 to 8.5 ft behind) and Trackman iO (3.3 to 3.5 ft in front).
- **About 16 ft:** Garmin R10 (6 to 8 ft behind), FlightScope Mevo Gen2 and Mevo+ (7 to 9 ft), Full Swing KIT (8 to 10 ft behind; 16 to 20 ft), Uneekor EYE XO2 and XR (13 by 16 by 10 ft recommended).
- **Not published:** the MLM2XPRO; assume the MLM2PRO's 15 ft.

Retailer radar guides run longer than the makers': 18 to 20 ft at Shop Indoor Golf, 19 ft or more at Carl's Place, and 19 to 21 ft for a Mevo Gen2 given the 13 ft of flight retailers recommend. PlayBetter's guides give a 10 by 10 by 9 ft room for the R50 and Square. A radar lane must also stay clear behind the golfer.

Ceiling height is the second wall: Square, the R50 and the GC3 list a 9 ft ceiling as the minimum, and overhead units want 9 to 10 ft. Overhead units serve both handers; the Launch Pro and Square Home have to be moved.

## The costs nobody prints

The software stack is what spec sheets leave off. Read October 18 to 19, 2026 (Northcourse) unless stated:

- **GSPro:** $250 a year, no lifetime option.
- **E6 Connect:** Basic $300 a year, Expanded $600, Home $1,000 perpetual.
- **Awesome Golf:** $14.99 a month, $159.99 a year or $349.99 lifetime. Not compatible with SkyTrak.

Then the maker's unlock fee:

- **Bushnell Launch Pro (Foresight+):** Free for ball data, Silver $199 a year, Gold $599 (quoted at $499 earlier in 2026). Bushnell says third-party software needs Gold, so GSPro runs about $849 a year.
- **Foresight GC3:** the sources disagree. Northcourse and Golf Simulator Source say current units need Gold ($599) for GSPro, with units holding a perpetual FSX Play or FSX 2020 license exempt. Breaking Eighty's June 2026 review says the GC3 needs no subscription. Confirm with Foresight before buying for GSPro.
- **Uneekor:** Pro at $199 a year is the connector, so GSPro is about $449 a year.
- **Rapsodo:** Core at $199.99 a year is required for spin, club data and third-party sims, so GSPro is about $449.99 a year. Studios is $399.99.
- **SkyTrak:** Essential $99.99, Core: Foresight $299.99, Elite $599.99 a year. None adds GSPro. Tier names and prices changed twice in 2025 to 2026.
- **Trackman iO:** first year of software included, then about $700 a year (Home Complete $1,100). No third-party software.

These carry no maker fee for third-party software: Garmin R10 and R50, FlightScope Mevo Gen2 (the data says the Mevo+ reaches GSPro with the Pro Package), Square Home and Omni, ProTee VX, and Full Swing KIT. For those, a year of GSPro is $250.

One-time costs: the Mevo Gen2 Pro Package ($1,000 list) and Face Impact Location ($499), and the ProTee VX connector for E6 and TGC ($295). Gaming PCs, ceiling installs, marked balls and stickers are extra; the data prices none of those.

## If the bay has to fold away

Room depth is the whole point of a bay that folds, because the monitor has to fit when the car or the living room comes back. Take 5 ft as the bay depth and set it against the placements above.

Radar is tight. The SC4 Pro sits 5 ft behind the ball, the whole 5 ft, before 8 ft of flight. The MLM2PRO (6.5 to 8.5 ft), R10 (6 to 8 ft), Mevo Gen2 and Mevo+ (7 to 9 ft) and KIT (8 to 10 ft) each need more than 5 ft behind the ball. The data says the MLM2PRO does not fit a 5 ft deep bay unless the unit sits outside the enclosure.

Camera units have no lane behind the golfer. They sit 1 to 2 ft beside the ball, so a shallow bay adds nothing to their lane: R50 1.5 ft to the side, Square Home 42 cm, ST MAX 12 to 15 in, GC3 about 2 ft. Several are easy to put away: the GC3 is light and battery powered, the EYE MINI and Omni carry batteries, and the R50 is one 9 lb box. The EYE MINI LITE is wired and stationary.

An overhead mount stays on the ceiling when the bay folds, and wants 9 to 10 ft (VX 9 ft, iO 9 ft 4 in or more). It belongs to the room, not to a freestanding bay with a 7 ft 6 in screen top, so our reading is that overhead suits a permanent room.

StowGolf is a fold-flat, freestanding bay with these published facts: 10 ft wide, 5 ft deep, 7 ft 6 in to the screen top, folds flat against the wall, list $2,495, and the first 25 at $2,295, arriving in December and offered to a free email list first. Against those figures, camera units are the ones with no lane behind the golfer. Nets and other fold-away frames are in [the enclosures guide](/blog/golf-simulator-enclosures).
